## 🩺 About Me

I'm a **Registered Nurse** building a career at the intersection of **healthcare, data, and technology**.

My clinical background has given me firsthand exposure to patient care, clinical workflows, healthcare documentation, and the realities behind healthcare information.

I'm now developing the technical side of that perspective through **SQL, PostgreSQL, Python, data analytics, database management, and healthcare technology**.

What interests me most is not technology for its own sake.

It's what happens when technology is applied to a real healthcare problem.

**How do we structure healthcare information?
How do we query it?
What can the data tell us?
How do we protect it?
And how can those insights contribute to better healthcare decisions?**

That's the space I'm building toward.
